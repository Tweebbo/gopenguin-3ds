# GoPenguin 1.4.2

- Penguin Style from January 2013 to December 2014 and the Monster Maker catalogue from the 2011 Halloween Party are in the catalogue list, with their original covers and pages. Pages download when you open them.
- Four free Penguin Cup vuvuzelas wait by the drinks table at the Stadium: Hot Sauce, Fluffies, Space Squids and Sharks. Walk up and press A beside one.
- Items with special actions play them again. Dance with a vuvuzela to blow it and wave your team flag; 65 more original item animations are included.
- The custom T-shirt and hoodie builders in past issues sell the colour and design you picked.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Your saved penguin is retained.
