# GoPenguin 1.4.6

- Penguin Style June and July 2014: the soccer kit builder works. Pick the pattern, colour and stripes, then buy any of the 32 kits.
- Penguin Style March and April 2014: the Muppet face builder works. Choose the hair, eyes and face, then buy your face.
- Igloos: the Dance Floor flooring lights up in changing colours, as in the original. The plasma ball, the purple and rocketship lava lamps, the aquarium, the TVs, the popcorn machine, laser lights and other animated furniture now move.
- Ice Fishing: sharks, boots and fleeing fish no longer flash across the screen for a moment when they reset. The same applies to the other games.
- Card-Jitsu: beating Sensei as a black belt gives the Ninja Mask and the Ninja Master stamp. Ninjas who missed the mask receive it after their next match.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Your saved penguin is retained.

See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).
