# Release notes

## 1.4.4

- Ice Fishing no longer slows down while the puffle swims across with the level tip. Long animated text is laid out once instead of every frame, which also keeps the PSA missions at a steady frame rate on Old 3DS.
- Find on a penguin's card answers when your buddy is offline or playing a game, instead of doing nothing.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Your saved penguin is retained.

See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).

## 1.4.2

- Penguin Style from January 2013 to December 2014 and the Monster Maker catalogue from the 2011 Halloween Party are in the catalogue list, with their original covers and pages. Pages download when you open them.
- Four free Penguin Cup vuvuzelas wait by the drinks table at the Stadium: Hot Sauce, Fluffies, Space Squids and Sharks. Walk up and press A beside one.
- Items with special actions play them again. Dance with a vuvuzela to blow it and wave your team flag; 65 more original item animations are included.
- The custom T-shirt and hoodie builders in past issues sell the colour and design you picked.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Your saved penguin is retained.

See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).

## 1.4.1

- Astro Barrier and Puffle Rescue open on their game again instead of an empty screen.
- Puffle Rescue no longer flickers, and both screens of the dual-screen minigames draw the same moment.
- Cart Surfer controls no longer lock up when Left or Right overlaps another button, and a direction held during a trick counts in the next corner.
- The custom T-shirt in Penguin Style can be bought again.
- Puffles no longer walk across walls when you come back from a minigame.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Your saved penguin is retained.

PC and mobile also receive Penguin Style 2013 and 2014, the Monster Maker catalogue, the Stadium vuvuzelas and the original item animations. See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).

## 1.3.6

- Halloween is over. Town, the Coffee Shop, the Dance Club, the Gift Shop, the map, the music and the Town poster are back to normal.
- Fixes puffles that stayed invisible while other art was downloading.
- Walking puffles keep their last pose while the next one loads, move smoothly and arrive beside their owner in each room.
- Puffles return home in your igloo, and puffle buttons recover after a lost server reply.
- Puffles of other players are hidden with Hide other penguins.
- Snowballs thrown into the Cave Mine funnel power the light machine again.
- Penguin Style now shows the November catalogue.
- The newspaper has been withdrawn.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Keep an SD backup and your account recovery code.

PC receives the same Halloween removal, catalogue, Cave Mine and puffle arrival changes. See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).

Host and Old 3DS profile emulator checks passed. Physical-console installation remains unverified.

## 1.3.5

- Adds the original special outfit actions, including the pizza outfit and mining gear.
- Adds Cave Mine digging with 10 coins per normal reward and a rare 100-coin reward. Move after five rewards to keep digging.
- Makes quick Pizzatron topping taps and sauce contact register more reliably, with less repeated graphics preparation.
- Makes small catalogue Buy buttons easier to tap. Catalogue cameras ignore Circle Pad drift and retain their position while tapping, dragging and zooming.
- Recovers a saved penguin from valid SD backup files when the main identity file is damaged. If recovery is not possible, the game asks for a restore instead of creating another penguin automatically.
- Includes the memory allocator correction from libctru 2.7.1.
- Offers smaller CIA and 3DSX downloads from 1.3.4, with complete packages as fallback.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Keep an SD backup and your account recovery code. Versions 1.3.1 and 1.3.2 require a fresh CIA or 3DSX installation.

PC also receives the outfit actions, mining and faster repeat room visits. See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).

Build, package, host, isolated multiplayer and Old/New 3DS emulator checks passed. Physical-console installation and touch acceptance remain unverified.


## 1.3.4

- Restores bottom-screen icons and the Halloween rooms, map, poster and music.
- Improves clothing loading and rendering, with a cache that adapts to available memory.
- Adds Help > Graphics > Hide other penguins, off by default. Chat and the player list remain available.
- Registers chat letters on touch-down and caches unchanged input layout.
- Offers incremental downloads from 1.3.3, 1.3.0, 1.2.5 and 1.2.3 for CIA and 3DSX.

See the [1.3.4 release details](RELEASE-1.3.4.md) for controls, update instructions and test limitations.

## 1.3.3

- Fixes GoPenguin stopping at startup on versions 1.3.1 and 1.3.2.
- Restores Agent Mission 1, the mission interface and the snowball artwork.
- Turn your seated penguin with the C-Stick, or by holding B and using the Circle Pad, D-Pad or map.

If you have 1.3.1 or 1.3.2, the game cannot update itself. Install the latest CIA or 3DSX again. Your penguin is kept.

Older releases are listed on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Tweebbo/gopenguin-3ds/releases). The [player changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/) covers every platform.
