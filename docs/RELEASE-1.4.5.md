# GoPenguin 1.4.5

- Treasure Hunt is open at the tables in the Captain's Quarters. Dig with a buddy and share the coins and gems you find.
- Offline mode: play without a connection and furnish a full offline igloo. Your online igloo is not changed.
- Placing furniture in your igloo shows the allowed spots and a close view that stays inside the room. Large igloos no longer report false save failures.
- The Starter Deck opens from your inventory, and adding buddies is easier to find and to answer.
- Old 3DS: page 9 of the May 2014 catalogue (and page 19 of June 2014) no longer crashes the console. The Phoning Facility runs at about 25 FPS instead of 10, and the costume catalogue is faster.
- When the Card-Jitsu menu from Sensei cannot load, the game frees memory and tries once more.
- Puffle Rescue shows its original intro art on the title screen, and Play and Exit no longer overlap.

Install the optional update from Help > Account > Updates. Your saved penguin is retained.

See the [full changelog](https://gopenguin.lol/changelog/).
